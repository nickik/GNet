# GNET-P — superseded trunk name

`GNET-P`, `GNet Link/10`, and `GNet Link/25` were provisional names for point-to-point infrastructure links.

The canonical technical family is now **[[GNet Carrier Trunk|GCT]]**. The first owned coaxial profiles are `GCT10-CX`, `GCT25-CX`, and later `GCT50-CX`; leased-carrier variants use bearer-oriented suffixes such as `GCT-T1`.

This note is retained for historical links only. **GNet-10** continues to mean the separate 10 Mbit/s switched LAN PHY.
